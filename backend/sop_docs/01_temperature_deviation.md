# SOP-01: Cargo Temperature Deviation

## Section 1 - Purpose
This procedure defines the required response when refrigerated cargo
temperature deviates from its contractual setpoint during transit.

## Section 2 - Deviation Thresholds
- **Minor deviation (0-2°C above setpoint):** Log the event. No action
  required beyond continued monitoring.
- **Moderate deviation (2-6°C above setpoint):** Increase telemetry sampling
  frequency and notify the fleet operations desk. Evaluate route change to
  reduce transit time.
- **Severe deviation (greater than 6°C above setpoint) or a sustained rising
  trend across consecutive readings:** Treat as an active refrigeration
  failure. The shipment must be classified High Risk and a divert-to-facility
  recommendation must be generated for human approval.

## Section 3 - Required Evidence
Any automated recommendation must cite the measured cargo temperature, the
contractual setpoint, and the deviation magnitude. Recommendations without
this evidence must not be acted upon.

## Section 4 - Escalation
Severe deviations require human approval before diversion. Do not divert a
truck automatically; route the recommendation to a fleet operations manager
for approval within 15 minutes of detection.
