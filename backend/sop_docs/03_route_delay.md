# SOP-03: Route Delay Impact on Cold Chain Integrity

## Section 1 - Purpose
Route delays extend the time cargo spends in transit and, combined with
temperature or weather risk factors, increase the probability of spoilage
before arrival.

## Section 2 - Delay Thresholds
- **Under 20 minutes:** Negligible cold-chain impact.
- **20-60 minutes:** Moderate impact. Consider a route change if the
  refrigeration unit is already under strain (temperature deviation or
  extreme heat present).
- **Over 60 minutes:** Significant impact. Treat as a material factor in the
  risk score, in addition to any temperature deviation.

## Section 3 - Route Change vs Diversion
A route change (selecting a faster path to the original destination) is
appropriate when the deviation is moderate and the refrigeration unit is
still able to hold temperature within a recoverable margin. Diversion to a
refrigerated facility is required instead when the deviation is severe or
the estimated remaining transit time exceeds the cargo's safe-exposure
window.

## Section 4 - Approval
Both a route change and a diversion are significant actions and require
human approval before execution. Continuing the current route without
modification does not require approval.
