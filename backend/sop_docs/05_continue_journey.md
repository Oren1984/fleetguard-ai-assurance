# SOP-05: Conditions for Continuing the Journey Without Action

## Section 1 - Purpose
Defines when a shipment may continue on its original route without a route
change or diversion.

## Section 2 - Conditions
A shipment may continue unmodified when all of the following hold:
- Cargo temperature deviation from setpoint is under 2°C.
- No sustained rising temperature trend across recent readings.
- External weather, where available, is not classified as extreme heat.
- Route delay, if any, is under 20 minutes.

## Section 3 - No Approval Required
Continuing the journey is not a significant action under this framework and
does not require human approval. It must still be logged with the
supporting evidence so the decision is auditable.

## Section 4 - Monitoring
Even Low Risk shipments continue to be monitored at the normal telemetry
interval so that a developing deviation is caught early.
