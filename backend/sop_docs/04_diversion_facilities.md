# SOP-04: Approved Refrigerated Diversion Facilities

## Section 1 - Purpose
Lists the pre-approved refrigerated facilities that a High Risk shipment may
be diverted to. Only facilities on this list may be selected by the
automated recommendation; any other destination requires manual selection by
a fleet operations manager.

## Section 2 - Approved Facility Network
| Facility ID | Name                          | Region        |
|-------------|-------------------------------|---------------|
| FAC-01      | Northgate Cold Storage        | North         |
| FAC-02      | Riverside Refrigerated Depot  | Central       |
| FAC-03      | Southbay Cold Chain Hub       | South         |
| FAC-04      | Eastline Distribution Facility| East          |
| FAC-05      | Westpoint Frozen Logistics    | West          |

## Section 3 - Diversion Procedure
1. Identify the region of the truck's current location.
2. Select the approved facility for that region.
3. Generate a diversion recommendation citing the facility ID and name.
4. Route the recommendation for human approval before contacting the
   facility or rerouting the driver.

## Section 4 - Evidence Requirement
A diversion recommendation without an identified approved facility is
incomplete and must not be presented as actionable; in that case the system
must state that evidence is insufficient to recommend a specific facility.
